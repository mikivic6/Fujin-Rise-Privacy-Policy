Fujin Rise — Privacy Policy

Fujin Rise does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connectivity of its own. High scores, unlocked themes, and settings are saved only on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The app offers one optional in-app purchase ("Pro"), processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store your payment information — Apple handles the transaction and confirms the purchase back to the app.

If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it.
